4. Data Minimisation and Local Data Storage

Use of the application does not require registration or the creation of a user account. No analytics, tracking, or advertising services (such as Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics) are integrated into the application.

All user-related data—including individual app settings, downloaded audio or text content, game progress, and your last used location—is stored exclusively locally within the sandboxed application storage on your device. No synchronisation with external cloud services takes place. You may delete this local data permanently at any time by clearing the application data in your device's system settings or by uninstalling the application.

5. Processing of Location Data

To calculate local prayer times and determine the direction of prayer (Qibla), the application requires location information:

a) GPS Location (Consent pursuant to Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR):

If you grant the application access to your location via your device's system permissions, your GPS coordinates are processed solely locally on your device. Your GPS data is never transmitted to the Data Controller, the developer, or any third parties. You may withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future via your device's system settings (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

b) Manual City Search (Legitimate Interest pursuant to Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR):

If you deny GPS access and manually enter the name of a city instead, this search query, together with your IP address, is transmitted directly from your device to the geocoding API of Open-Meteo (https://open-meteo.com) in order to retrieve the corresponding geographic coordinates. The legal basis for this processing is our legitimate interest in providing a user-friendly alternative for location determination without requiring GPS access.

6. Local Notifications

Prayer time reminders are scheduled and triggered exclusively locally using your device's operating system functions. No push tokens are generated, and no data is transmitted to external push notification servers (such as Google Firebase Cloud Messaging).

7. Integration of External Services and Content (Third-Party Providers)

To provide up-to-date content (such as association news, religious texts, audio files, or precious metal prices), the application establishes direct HTTP/HTTPS connections to the servers of the respective content providers whenever an active internet connection is available. For technical reasons, it is necessary to transmit your IP address and technical metadata (e.g., operating system version, timestamp of the request) to the respective servers, as the content cannot otherwise be delivered to your device.

This processing is based on our legitimate interest in the secure, efficient, and error-free provision of the application's functionalities (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR):

  • Association News ("Aktuelles"): GitHub, Inc. (https://githubusercontent.com)
